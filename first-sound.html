First sound

If you cannot get a tone yet, you are normal. It can take days or weeks of short daily tries before the embouchure clicks. No shortcuts — only calm experiments.

Do not blow harder when nothing comes out. Harder air makes hiss, not music. Softer and more focused wins.

Hear a real bansuri long tone at your Sa, then hunt that colour. Full plan: practice routine.

Warm up without the flute

  1. Sit or stand tall. Shoulders soft. Breathe into the belly, not puffed cheeks.
  2. Blow on hot tea — a thin, steady stream. That is the shape you want.
  3. In a mirror: lips form a small oval about the size of a drinking-straw opening.

Straw drill

Teachers often use a straw before bamboo:

  1. Hold a thin straw gently between relaxed lips — do not bite.
  2. Blow slow, even air. Cheeks stay flat.
  3. Aim across the edge of your other palm — feel where air hits.
  4. Nudge the angle up or down. Notice how direction changes.

When that stream is steady, aim it across the bansuri blowing edge the same way.

Kiss-and-roll

  1. Press lips gently together.
  2. Kiss the flute so the blowing hole is against the lips; feel the near edge on the middle of the lower lip.
  3. Roll the flute away about a quarter turn so the hole faces more toward the ceiling — lower lip still covers a little of the near rim.
  4. Blow a soft whistle-stream across the far edge, not straight down the tube.

On the flute — first tone

  1. Leave finger holes open at first (fingers hovering). Less to leak.
  2. Support so the flute does not wobble.
  3. Blow 3–5 seconds. Listen for any pitch under the air noise.
  4. Micro-adjust: roll in/out, slide on the lip, change aperture — one change at a time.
  5. When a tone appears, hold it. Do not add fingers yet.
Higher notes later need a smaller aperture and faster air — not simply louder blowing.

No sound? Checklist

  • Air into the hole instead of across the edge → roll out slightly.
  • Lips too wide → smaller oval.
  • Cheeks puffed → reset; support from the belly.
  • Flute shaking → stabilize before fingering.
  • Only two frustrated minutes → stop; try again tomorrow.
  • Still stuck after a week of short daily tries → have a player watch your angle once.
