Hold & posture

The bansuri is side-blown. Right-handed setup is standard in most tutorials: flute extends to your right.

Hands (right-handed)

Left hand covers the upper three holes (nearest the blowing hole): index, middle, ring. Thumb supports from underneath.

Right hand covers the lower three: index, middle, ring. Thumb balances from below.

Cover holes with fleshy pads so no air leaks — a tiny leak kills the tone.

Balance

Light clamp, not death grip. Elbows free. Sit tall so breath stays open.
