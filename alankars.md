# Simple alankars

      Alankars are short note patterns that train fingers and breath together.

    
    
      ## Start here (slow)

      
        
          - Sa Re Ga Re Sa

          - Re Ga Ma Ga Re

          - Ga Ma Pa Ma Ga

          - Ma Pa Dha Pa Ma

          - Sa Re Ga Ma Pa Dha Ni Sa — then reverse down

        
        Clarity beats speed. Add the metronome only after the fingers know the path.
