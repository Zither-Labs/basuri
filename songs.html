Simple songs

Familiar tunes in sargam so you practise real phrases, not only drills. Playback uses real bansuri samples tuned to your Sa. M = tivra Ma (all open) unless noted.

These are teaching sketches (melody outline), not full classical bandish. Sing or hum once, then play.

Twinkle Twinkle (sargam)

S S P P D D P · M M G G R R S

Line 1. P = middle Pa (top open / five closed). D = Dha above that Pa. Match the playback, then copy slowly.

Full sketch

P P M M G G R · (repeat) · then first line again

Happy Birthday (opening)

S S R S M G · S S R S P M · S S S' D P M G · N N D P M G R S

Simplified contour for practice — listen, then match the shape. S' = upper Sa (same fingering, faster air).
