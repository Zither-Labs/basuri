# Learn Basuri — Practice routine

Short daily sessions. Use real bansuri clips as a **target tone**, then hunt that colour on your bamboo.
Interactive version: [routine.html](routine.html).

## Goal

Never blow harder when there is no tone. Soft, focused air across the edge.

## 1 · Hear the target (1–2 min)

1. Set Sa on the practice bar to match your three-hole Sa.
2. Play Sa sample; hum it; notice the bamboo colour.
3. Optional: play a short real phrase for colour only.

## 2 · Daily block (15–20 min)

### A · Body & straw (3 min)
Tall sit, soft shoulders, belly breath. Straw stream on the palm.
→ [Straw drill](first-sound.html#straw)

### B · Kiss-and-roll hunt (5–7 min)
Holes open. Soft air across the edge. One change at a time.
Max two frustrated minutes — pause, replay Sa, try again.
→ [Kiss-and-roll](first-sound.html#kiss)

### C · Lock the tone (5 min) — when a tone appears
Hold 3–5 seconds × ~10. Then top-three Sa vs drone.
Pattern: S · S · S · S · S

### D · Tiny melody (3–5 min) — only if Sa is stable
One easy alankar (e.g. S R G R S) or song line.
→ [Alankars](alankars.html) · [Songs](songs.html)

## 3 · Week map

- **Days 1–3** — A–B only. Success = any pitch under the hiss.
- **Days 4–7** — Add C; match drone Sa.
- **Week 2** — Add D; learn middle Pa (0XXXXX).
- **Week 3+** — Songs + one komal drill a week; long tones every day.
