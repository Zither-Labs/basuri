Breath & long tones

Clear bansuri tone is mostly breath control. Long tones train steady air before fancy fingers.

How

  1. Start the practice-bar drone on Sa.
  2. Play any comfortable note you can sound.
  3. Hold 5–8 seconds evenly. Rest. Repeat.
  4. Grow toward 10–12 seconds.
  5. If the tone cracks, shrink the lip opening; do not blast.

10-minute starter

  • 2 min — straw or mirror embouchure
  • 4 min — long tones on your best note
  • 4 min — gently close holes once the tone is stable
