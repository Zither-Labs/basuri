      ## Quick terms

      
        
          - **Embouchure** — lip shape and angle on the blowing hole

          - **Sa** — tonic; match the drone

          - **Sargam** — Sa Re Ga Ma Pa Dha Ni

          - **Meend** — glide between notes (later skill)

          - **Basuri / bansuri** — bamboo flute (spelling varies)
