Finger ladder — one step at a time

Do not learn all seven notes at once. Climb one finger change per day (or per session). After Ma the ladder changes shape — that bridge is the whole point of this page.

Eastern map: Sa = top three holes covered. Early M = tivra Ma (all open). Check with the Sa drone and Listen.

The path at a glance

  1. Sa — three closed · lift nothing
  2. Re — lift one finger (hole 3, farthest of the top three)
  3. Ga — lift one more (hole 2)
  4. Ma♯ — lift the last top finger (hole 1) · all open
  5. Pa — stop lifting · new fingering (see bridge)
  6. Dha → Ni → Sa' — open from the bottom or overblow Sa

Rule: Sa→Re→Ga→Ma is “open one more hole from the top.” Pa is not “open one more” — there is no hole left. You reset.

Rung 1 · Sa only (lock the home)

Fingers: top three closed · bottom three open · XXXOOO

One job: match the practice-bar drone. No other notes yet.

  • Hold 4–5 seconds · rest · repeat ×10
  • If the tone dies when you cover holes, seal pads fully (mirror helps)

Rung 5 · After Ma — the Pa bridge

Stop “opening one more hole.” After all-open Ma there is nothing left to lift. Pa is a new shape, not the next open hole.

Pick one Pa method (stay with it for a week)

MethodCodeWhat you do
A · Teacher fork (recommended if your guru uses it) 0XXXXX From Ma (all open): close holes 2–6, leave hole 1 open. Or from Sa: close bottom three, then open only the top hole.
B · Overblow XXXXXX Cover all six (same as mandra Pa), but use faster / smaller air so the higher Pa speaks. Check a clean fifth vs Sa drone.
Beginner soften half-open top Same as A but crack the top hole — often easier than fully open.

Detail: why these are Pa.

Transition drill (Ma → Pa → Ma)

Do this slowly with eyes on the fingers — it is a reset, not a slide of one finger.

  1. Play Ma (all open).
  2. Freeze air for a split second · plant the Pa fingering (A or B).
  3. Blow Pa · match drone (fifth above Sa).
  4. Return to all-open Ma · then home to Sa.
Climb through the bridge
