# Learn Basuri — Simple songs

Familiar tunes in sargam. Playback on [songs.html](songs.html) uses real bansuri samples
tuned to your Sa. **M** = tivra Ma unless noted.

## Twinkle Twinkle

S S P P D D P · M M G G R R S

Then: P P M M G G R (repeat) · return to first line.

## Happy Birthday (opening sketch)

S S R S M G · S S R S P M · S S S' D P M G · N N D P M G R S

## Saregama

- Aroha: S R G M P D N S'
- Avaroha: S' N D P M G R S

## Om Jai Jagdish (opening sketch)

S S S R G · G R S ,N S  
G G G M P · P M G R S

Teaching outlines only — learn full tunes from a singer or teacher.
