# Flute layout calculator

Three modes in the live tool ([make.html](make.html)):

1. **Bamboo bansuri** — side-blown; cork + mouth hole; 6 fingers + optional pancham  
2. **PVC bansuri** — same craft hole %; Schedule-40 bores; end cap; **never heat PVC**  
3. **Murali** — end-blown fipple; marks from labium/window; 6 holes; voicing first  

Sa on side-blown maps = **top three finger holes covered** (Eastern). Starting marks only — finish by ear ([Listen](listen.html)).

## Modes at a glance

| | Bamboo | PVC | Murali |
|--|--------|-----|--------|
| Blow | Side mouth hole | Side mouth hole | Into tip (duct) |
| Tube | Seasoned bamboo | Sch.40 + cap | Bamboo (+ block) |
| Holes | 6 + pancham | Same % | 6 (no pancham here) |

See also [The murali](murali.html) for embouchure culture vs basuri.

## Outputs

Length, ID/wall (PVC snapped to ½″–1¼″ Sch.40), mouth or labium position, hole table (mm/in, gaps, diameters), diagram, and a mode-specific workflow.

## Safety

Eye protection. Bamboo: optional hot rods. **PVC: drill/file only — no flame.** Adults only unless supervised.
