Alankars with playback

Short note patterns for fingers and breath. Press Play to hear a real bansuri sample pitched to your Sa (practice bar below). Then echo on your flute.

M in early patterns = tivra Ma (all holes open). Shuddha Ma is written m. Komal notes use lowercase r g d n. See komal & tivra. High Pa / Dha / Ni forks: high register & alternates.

Hear a real bansuri first

Unprocessed phrase recordings (not pitched to your Sa) — tone colour to aim for.

Komal taste (half-hole)

10 · Komal Re

S r R r S

Half-open the 3rd hole for r.

11 · Komal Ga

R g G g R

12 · Shuddha Ma vs tivra Ma

S R G m M G R S

m = half-hole shuddha Ma · M = all-open tivra Ma.

How to practise these

  1. Turn on the Sa drone. Match your three-hole Sa to it before any pattern.
  2. Play the sample once while watching the lit swaras.
  3. Echo on flute — same speed or slower. Clarity before metro.
  4. Two clean rounds, then the next card. Stop while tone is still honest.
